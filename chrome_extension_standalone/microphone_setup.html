🎤 Microphone Setup Required

Follow these steps to enable voice control

1 Look for the Icon in Address Bar
When you click "Start Voice Control", Chrome will show a camera/microphone icon 🎤 in the address bar (next to the URL).
📍 Address bar → Look for 🎤 or 📷 icon
2 Click the Icon and Allow Access
Click the microphone icon and select:
  • "Always allow" or "Allow"
  • Make sure your microphone is selected
✅ Select "Always allow" for this extension
3 Test Your Microphone
After allowing access:
  1. Click the extension icon in your toolbar
  2. Click "Start Voice Control"
  3. Badge should turn GREEN 🟢
  4. Say: "scroll down"
💡Tip: If Chrome doesn't show the microphone icon, try:
  • Go to: chrome://settings/content/microphone
  • Make sure microphone is not blocked
  • Add this extension to "Allow" list
  • Reload the extension and try again
